What makes a page of text feel calm?

Assistant

Mostly restraint. Calm comes from a few decisions made consistently, rather than many made loudly:

  • A comfortable measure, around 60 to 70 characters a line.
  • Generous, regular leading so the eye finds the next line.
  • One accent colour, used only where it means something.

Whitespace does the rest.

You

Show me that rhythm in CSS.

Assistant

Set it once on the container and let everything inherit it:

.prose {
  max-width: 65ch;
  line-height: 1.6;
  margin-block: 1.5rem;
}

Then keep every margin a multiple of the line height.
